## CI Platforms

Full-featured competitive intelligence platforms with monitoring, analysis, and distribution.

- [Crayon](https://crayon.co) - Enterprise CI platform with battlecards and sales enablement. Best for large sales teams.
- [Klue](https://klue.com) - Competitive enablement with win/loss analysis. Strong battlecard builder.
- [Kompyte](https://kompyte.com) - Automated competitor tracking with battlecards. Part of Semrush.
- [Contify](https://contify.com) - Market and competitive intelligence with custom taxonomy.
- [GetAppNiche](https://getappniche.com/) - App Store competitive intelligence for indie iOS founders: revenue estimates, downloads, ASO keywords, ads, and reviews.
- [IntelCue](https://intelcue.ai) - AI-powered competitor monitoring with automated digests. Built for lean teams.
- [AlphaSense](https://alpha-sense.com) - Enterprise market intelligence with financial data focus.
- [Valona](https://valona.io) - Strategic intelligence platform for enterprises.

## Website Monitoring

Track changes to competitor websites, pricing pages, and product updates.

- [Visualping](https://visualping.io) - Visual website change detection with screenshots.
- [Changd](https://changd.io) - Website monitoring with Slack/email alerts.
- [Distill.io](https://distill.io) - Browser extension and app for page monitoring.
- [Versionista](https://versionista.com) - Track changes across multiple pages.
- [Fluxguard](https://fluxguard.com) - Website change monitoring for teams.

## SEO & Traffic Analysis

Analyze competitor search rankings, traffic, and content strategy.

- [Semrush](https://semrush.com) - Comprehensive SEO toolkit with competitor analysis.
- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) - Backlink analysis and keyword research.
- [SimilarWeb](https://similarweb.com) - Website traffic and engagement estimates.
- [SpyFu](https://spyfu.com) - Competitor keyword and PPC research.
- [Moz](https://moz.com) - SEO tools with domain authority metrics.
- [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com) - SEO platform with competitor tracking.

## News & Media Monitoring

Track competitor press releases, news mentions, and media coverage.

- [Feedly](https://feedly.com) - RSS reader with AI-powered filtering.
- [Meltwater](https://meltwater.com) - Media intelligence platform.
- [Cision](https://cision.com) - PR and media monitoring.

- [NewsWhip](https://newswhip.com) - Predictive media intelligence.

## Company & Funding Data

Research competitor funding, leadership, and company structure.

- [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com) - Private market data for investors.
- [Owler](https://owler.com) - Company insights and competitive tracking.
- [ZoomInfo](https://zoominfo.com) - B2B contact and company data.
- [LinkedIn Sales Navigator](https://linkedin.com/sales) - Company and employee insights.
- [CB Insights](https://cbinsights.com) - Market intelligence and company data.

## Review Monitoring

Track what customers say about competitors on review sites.

- [Capterra](https://capterra.com) - Software reviews and directory.
- [TrustRadius](https://trustradius.com) - In-depth B2B software reviews.

## Job Posting Trackers

Monitor competitor hiring to understand strategy and investment areas.

- [LinkedIn Jobs](https://linkedin.com/jobs) - Largest job board, shows hiring trends.
- [Glassdoor](https://glassdoor.com) - Jobs plus company reviews and salaries.
- [Indeed](https://indeed.com) - Aggregated job listings.
- [Otta](https://otta.com) - Startup job board with company insights.
- [Theirstack](https://theirstack.com) - Track competitor job postings and tech stacks.

## Books

Essential reading on competitive intelligence.

- **"Competitive Intelligence Advantage"** by Seena Sharp - Practical CI fundamentals.
- **"The Art of War"** by Sun Tzu - Timeless strategy principles.
- **"Competitive Strategy"** by Michael Porter - Framework for competitive analysis.
- **"Blue Ocean Strategy"** by W. Chan Kim - Finding uncontested market space.
- **"Obviously Awesome"** by April Dunford - Positioning against competitors.
- **"Playing to Win"** by A.G. Lafley - Strategic decision-making framework.
